Meet the Candidate

Marco Rubio

From Miami to the United States Senate—a story of family, freedom, and public service.

Senator Marco Rubio has spent his career fighting for working families, defending American interests overseas, and proving that bold ideas still have a place in our politics.

The son of Cuban immigrants, Rubio grew up in Miami and learned early that freedom and hard work can change a family's story. He served in the Florida House of Representatives, including as Speaker, before representing Florida in the United States Senate.

Now he brings that same experience to a run for the White House: a proven record on foreign policy, bipartisan results on issues that matter to voters, and a message centered on restoring confidence in America's future.

Policy Priorities

The Rubio Platform

Kitchen-table priorities backed by a real Senate record—short on talk, long on solutions.

Economy & Cost of Living

66% of Americans call inflation a very big problem.

  • Cut federal barriers to domestic energy production.
  • Target tax relief for working families.
  • Review policies that drive up grocery and everyday goods prices.

Immigration & Border Security

38% of Americans—and 60% of Republicans—call illegal immigration a very big problem.

  • Expand border surveillance technology.
  • Prioritize removing undocumented immigrants convicted of serious violent crimes.
  • Hire immigration judges to clear case backlogs.
Upholding the Law at Our Border Act — Co-sponsored; requires DHS inspector general reviews of migrant screening on the southwest border.
Protect Our Communities from DUIs Act — Makes certain DUI offenses grounds for immigration inadmissibility and deportation.

Healthcare & Affordability

73% call healthcare affordability a very big problem.

  • Require hospitals and insurers to disclose negotiated prices.
  • Regulate pharmacy benefit managers for transparency and pass-through savings on drugs.
At Home Services Act — Bipartisan pilot for Medicare coverage of certain hospital-level observation care outside traditional hospitals.
RACE for Children Act — Helped expand pediatric cancer treatment research; provisions were signed into law.

Federal Debt & Spending

64% call the federal budget deficit a very big problem.

  • Regular agency spending audits and public annual deficit-reduction targets.
  • Shrink wasteful government and refocus on core responsibilities.
Balanced Budget Amendment — Original cosponsor of a proposed amendment requiring balanced annual budgets and capping spending at 18% of GDP (with limited exceptions).

Government Corruption & Accountability

74% say money in politics is a very big problem.

  • Ban individual stock trading by members of Congress and their spouses.
  • Faster disclosure of large political donations and independent expenditures.
STOCK Act — Voted to bar use of nonpublic congressional information for personal trades and to require financial disclosures.
Codification of Verified Values Act — Requires CVV verification for online political contributions to strengthen donation checks.

Drug Addiction & Fentanyl

55% call drug addiction a very big problem.

  • More fentanyl detection at ports of entry.
  • Stronger sanctions on trafficking networks.
  • Expand overdose-reversal meds and addiction treatment access.
Fentanyl felony murder bill — Introduced Senate legislation allowing federal felony murder charges when unlawful fentanyl distribution causes death (did not pass).

Housing Affordability

64% are very concerned about housing costs.

  • Incentivize local approval of starter homes.
  • Streamline permitting and reduce red tape on construction.
Home Advantage for American Families Act — Targets foreign purchases that push up U.S. residential real estate prices.
Housing Accountability Act — Surveys tenants in certain federally assisted housing to flag persistent property or management failures.

Foreign Policy & Security

  • Rebuild military readiness and support veterans.
  • Diplomacy backed by strength with allies worldwide.
  • Secure supply chains critical to national security.

Education & Families

  • Transparency and choice in K–12 education.
  • Apprenticeships and dual-enrollment pathways.
  • Parental empowerment in schools and online safety.

Source note

Public-opinion figures reflect AP Gov research materials for this hypothetical campaign. Endorsement reporting: Axios (July 8, 2026) on donor Ken Griffin’s preference in a potential 2028 GOP primary — read the article.
